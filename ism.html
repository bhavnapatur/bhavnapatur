Frisco ISD Advanced Academics

Independent Study & Mentorship

ISM is a selective elective for high-achieving juniors and seniors in Frisco ISD that focuses on real-world experience. To get in, students have to go through a competitive application and interview process. Once in the program, we spend the year conducting deep research and interviewing professionals in our chosen field. The goal is to eventually find a mentor who can provide hands-on guidance and help us gain actual professional experience before we even graduate high school.
